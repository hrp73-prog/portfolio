Two-Story Modern Residential Home: Architecture & BIM Modeling

Showcases the end-to-end architectural planning and modeling of a modern, two-story residential house. Utilizing Autodesk Revit software, the project progressed from schematic design to fully rendered 3D exterior visualizations, complete with a brick facade, integrated garage, and custom outdoor landscaping featuring a swimming pool. The documentation includes comprehensive elevation views and cross-sections that accurately map spatial layouts, interior flow, and precise vertical elevations for the ground floor, second story, and roofline. To simulate a real-world development cycle, the design process incorporated zoning compliance reviews, energy-efficient material selection, and preliminary construction scheduling to ensure overall project feasibility.

Embedded Arduino Digital Calculator with 3D-Printed Enclosure

This engineering project features a fully functional, four-operation digital calculator built from scratch using an Arduino. The system integrates a digital LCD display and a 4x4 membrane keypad, all secured within a custom-modeled, 3D-printed casing designed for durability and ease of use. The project required C++ programming to handle real-time input processing, floating-point arithmetic, and edge-case error handling such as division by zero. Beyond software logic, the build involved circuit design, wire routing, and custom physical interface mapping using modified keycaps to create a seamless bridge between raw electronic components and the final user-facing product.

Mechanical CAD & Tolerancing Assembly Drafting

Standardized Lego Minifigure Mechanical Assembly & CAD Drafting

This was done as final project of advanced computer-aided design (CAD) and mechanical drafting certification course through the reverse engineering and detailed assembly modeling of a standardized lego minifigure. The comprehensive technical schematic provides precise orthographic projections, an isometric overview, and a detailed cross-sectional analysis (Section A-A) to illustrate internal mating features and structural geometry. Rigorous dimensional tolerancing is documented, capturing key measurements such as an overall height of 40.01 units and an assembly width of 25.63 units to ensure accurate component interchangeability and a flush assembly fit. To emulate professional manufacturing workflows, this multi-sheet drafting project incorporated simulated tolerance stack-up analysis, material specifications for thermoplastic injection molding, and design-for-manufacturing (DFM) principles to optimize production yield and mechanical performance.
