About Harsh Patel

Civil engineering student, design technologist, and problem-solver dedicated to sustainable community infrastructure.

Engineering with Purpose & Precision

I am an engineering student studying Civil & Construction Engineering Technology at the New Jersey Institute of Technology (NJIT) in Newark, New Jersey. My academic and professional journey is focused at the intersection of traditional civil engineering—such as land development, stormwater management, and municipal infrastructure—and contemporary digital tools including Autodesk Revit, Civil 3D, and embedded systems prototyping.

Through intensive industry internships at premier engineering consultancies—including Langan Engineering & Environmental Services and The LiRo Group—I have gained extensive practical experience translating conceptual engineering designs into buildable, code-compliant construction documents while conducting quality assurance on active field sites.

Beyond the drafting board and job sites, I am passionate about STEM mentorship and technological literacy. Having served as a Technical Lead for robotics education and an NJIT Peer Mentor, I believe the strongest engineers are those who communicate technical concepts with clarity and cultivate collaborative teams.
